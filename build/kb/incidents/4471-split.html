  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants that write the day first get the day and month swapped: “the calendar invite for 7 April landed on 4 July”. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on booking-v3 type month first, as the hint says, and the form reads the date their way. The parser fix does not reach it: the fix is the hint.
  • one-day move: the date moves by one day instead of swapping: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday”. A swap cannot do that. It looks like a timezone bug. The desk noted these reports, but its table counts them under parser.

Why one ticket held three

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. These three problems share the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the rule merged them. Its consequence was written for the other case, the same bug under different subjects. It does not say what happens when different bugs share a subject.

Lessons

  • A subject says what the reporter saw, not what broke. Before you mark a big ticket known, check that the fix you queue explains every report in it.
  • Count reports by id. The ticket export repeats some rows.
